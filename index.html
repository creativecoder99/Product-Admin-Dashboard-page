Terms of Service

Last Modified: September 23, 2026

1. Purpose and Operational Scope

The Nexgenesis Product Administration Console provides authorized personnel with centralized tools to manage hardware product specifications, regional warehouse allocation, and pricing rules. Access is restricted to designated operators, distribution partners, and engineering teams.

2. User Accounts and Security

Users must maintain the confidentiality of administrative credentials. Operational actions, including catalog adjustments, pricing updates, and inventory transfers, are recorded in the audit trail with user identity timestamps.

3. Catalog Data and Accuracy

Product data published through this system must adhere to engineering tolerances and regional compliance guidelines. Demonstration metrics and prototype listings are marked accordingly within the catalog interface.

4. Data Portability and Backups

Authorized administrators may export catalog records in structured CSV or JSON formats. Imported files must validate against the official catalog schema prior to database application.

5. Disclaimers and Limitations

This software is provided for administrative coordination purposes. Simulated performance metrics, margin projections, and inventory velocity indicators represent mathematical models based on historical telemetry.

Privacy Policy

Last Modified: September 23, 2026

1. Information Collected

Within the administration environment, we collect operator email addresses, role permissions, session identifiers, and workstation timestamps to safeguard catalog operations and verify data provenance.

2. Local Storage and Client-Side Persistence

This application uses browser local storage (`localStorage`) to preserve catalog modifications, draft products, UI preferences, and authentication sessions across workstation reloads. Local storage entries may be erased at any time through the Settings panel.

3. Telemetry and Audit Records

System modifications such as price adjustments, purchase order issuances, and product deletions generate structured log entries to preserve inventory accountability.

4. Third Party Disclosures

Administrative telemetry is not sold or distributed to third party advertising networks. Operational data remains confined to internal logistics coordinators and distribution endpoints.
